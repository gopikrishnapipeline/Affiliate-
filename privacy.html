Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Daily Deal Discovery shares product recommendations. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website.

Information we collect

We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t collect your name, email or any other personal details on this website. Searching for a product number happens in your browser and isn’t sent to us.

Third-party services

To work, this page loads fonts and product information from outside services such as Google. These services may receive standard technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser connects to them. They have their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links

Some links on this website are affiliate links. If you buy something through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are useful.

Other websites

When you tap a product, you go to another website, such as an online store. That website has its own privacy policy and may use cookies. We aren’t responsible for how other websites handle your information.

Cookies

We don’t set cookies on this website. Third-party websites you visit through our links, and services we load, may set their own.

Children

This website isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit our Contact page.
